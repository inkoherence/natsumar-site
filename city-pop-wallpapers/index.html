Pastel Postcard is our take on the city pop look: flat color, clean lines and skies that never run out of blue. Whitewashed villas, lone palm trees, empty pools and vintage cars parked by the sea — scenes that feel like a record sleeve from a summer you half remember.

Every wallpaper is framed for a phone screen, so the horizon and the sky sit where your clock and icons need them. Save your favorites, or let the daily widget surprise you with a new one each morning.
