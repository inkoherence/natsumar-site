02 · Ink & Salt

Manga-style summer wallpapers

23 wallpapers in this style

Ink & Salt is the most graphic of our styles: confident ink lines, flat bright color and characters caught mid-summer (a glance over sunglasses, a swim at dusk, a walk along a harbour wall). It borrows the energy of manga covers and Japanese pop illustration.

Because every scene is built around a figure, they’re composed to keep faces clear of the clock. Use the preview to blur or dim the background before you save.
